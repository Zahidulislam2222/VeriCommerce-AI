# Code of conduct

The VeriCommerce AI project is maintained by Zahidul Islam. Participants should communicate respectfully, review work on its technical merits and make collaboration safe for people of different backgrounds and experience.

Expected behavior includes clear evidence, constructive criticism, respect for consent/privacy and prompt correction of mistakes. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual misconduct, doxxing, impersonation, disclosure of private records and repeated disruptive conduct are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to [muhammadzahidulislam2222@gmail.com](mailto:muhammadzahidulislam2222@gmail.com). Include the relevant interaction and requested safety needs; do not circulate sensitive evidence publicly. If the concern involves the maintainer, an independent appeal channel has not been established; that limitation is stated rather than inventing an enforcement committee.

The maintainer may request changes, remove harmful material, restrict participation or decline contributions proportionately. Reports are handled with limited necessary disclosure; absolute confidentiality or fixed response time cannot be promised. Security vulnerabilities use [the security policy](SECURITY.md).

This original policy applies to project-controlled contribution spaces and project-related interactions. It does not replace applicable law or authorize retaliation.
